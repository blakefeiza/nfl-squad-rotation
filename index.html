How each glyph is decided
  • Pie fill: the player's snaps divided by the team's offense, defense or special-teams snaps in that game. The right-hand column is the same share over every game so far.
  • Started: the first player listed at a depth-chart slot just before kickoff who actually played. If the listed starter was out, the next player who played counts as the starter.
  • Off the bench: played offense or defense snaps but did not start.
  • On the bench: on the roster and healthy, but took no snaps in that unit.
  • Hurt: out or doubtful on the injury report, on injured reserve, or inactive with an injury designation.
  • Offense and defense list players with at least one snap there, or who were hurt. Special teams lists everyone who took a special-teams snap, kickers, punters and long snappers included.
